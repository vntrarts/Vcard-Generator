NFC URLs are limited to 490 UTF-8 bytes to leave room for NFC record overhead within 500 bytes. Uploaded images cannot fit in this limit and are omitted from NFC links; use a hosted photo URL. The link opens this public site and displays the customer's card. The card data is encoded in the URL, not stored on a server. Anyone with the link can read it. A working NFC website link requires internet on the recipient's phone. The downloaded HTML works offline once transferred to their device.
